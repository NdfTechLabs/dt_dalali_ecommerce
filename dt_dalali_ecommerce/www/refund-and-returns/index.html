Refund & Returns Policy

Dalali Winehouse Ltd · Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Return and Refund Policy. This policy describes Dalali Winehouse's standard return and refund process. It does not remove or limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies that apply under applicable law.
1. Return window

Our standard return window is 15 days from the date you receive the item, subject to the conditions below and any mandatory rights provided by applicable law.

Because alcoholic beverages and other products may be subject to safety, quality and regulatory requirements, certain products or circumstances may have special return restrictions. We will explain any applicable restriction when you contact us.

2. Eligibility for a standard return

For a standard change-of-mind return, the item should generally be:

  • unused and in the condition in which it was received;
  • in its original packaging where reasonably applicable; and
  • accompanied by an order number, receipt or other proof of purchase.

We may decline a standard return where the item has been opened, consumed, damaged through customer misuse, or cannot reasonably be resold, subject always to any mandatory legal rights that apply to defective or incorrectly supplied goods.

3. Damaged, incorrect or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, defective or materially different from what you ordered, contact us promptly. Provide your order number and photographs or other evidence where possible.

After assessment, we may offer an appropriate remedy such as replacement, correction, repair where applicable, or refund, subject to the circumstances and applicable law.

4. How to request a return
  1. Contact customer service at customercare@dalaliwinehouse.com or +254 728 583 967.
  2. Provide your order number, the product concerned and the reason for the return.
  3. Where requested, provide photographs or other information needed to assess the item.
  4. Wait for return instructions before sending the item back.
5. Return shipping

For a standard change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for reasonable return shipping costs unless we agree otherwise.

Where the return is because we supplied an incorrect item or the item arrived damaged or defective, we will assess the appropriate shipping arrangement and remedy based on the circumstances and applicable law.

6. Refunds

Once a returned item has been received and assessed, we will communicate the outcome of the refund request. Where a refund is approved, it will normally be made to the original payment method where practicable, or by another appropriate method agreed with the customer.

For card or mobile-money payments, the time for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Where a refund is required because an order was cancelled before fulfilment or could not be supplied, we will arrange the applicable refund in accordance with the circumstances and applicable law.

7. Exceptions and legal rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive a consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded. Where applicable law provides a mandatory remedy for defective, unsafe, misrepresented or incorrectly supplied goods, that remedy will apply.

We may refuse returns that are fraudulent or that do not meet the conditions of the applicable return route, but we will not use this policy to remove mandatory statutory rights.

8. Product recalls and safety

If a product is subject to a safety notice or recall, we may contact affected customers and provide instructions for return, replacement or refund as appropriate. Customers should follow any safety instructions provided with or about a recalled product.

9. Contact us

Email: customercare@dalaliwinehouse.com
Phone / WhatsApp: +254 728 583 967
