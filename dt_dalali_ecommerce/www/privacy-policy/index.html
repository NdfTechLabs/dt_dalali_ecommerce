Privacy Policy

Dalali Winehouse Ltd · Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how Dalali Winehouse Ltd collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information when you use our website, create an account, place an order, contact us, or use Google Sign-In. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Customer Service Policy and Refund & Returns Policy.
1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Dalali Winehouse Ltd (“Dalali”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates the Dalali Winehouse online storefront and related customer services at dalaliwinehouseltd.com.

This policy applies to personal information collected through our website, customer accounts, orders, enquiries, customer support interactions and authentication services used by the website.

For privacy enquiries or requests to exercise your data rights, contact us using the customer-service details published on this website.

2. Legal purchase age and responsible access

Our products include alcoholic beverages. You must not use our services or provide personal information for the purpose of purchasing alcohol if you are below the legal purchase age applicable to the transaction and the jurisdiction in which you are accessing or purchasing from us.

We may use information necessary for age or access controls where required by law or our service configuration. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from persons who are not legally permitted to purchase alcohol.

3. Personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact information: name, email address, telephone number, billing or delivery address and similar contact details.
  • Account information: username, authentication information and account preferences. We do not receive your Google password when you use Google Sign-In.
  • Order information: products viewed, added to a basket or purchased, order details, delivery information, invoices and transaction references.
  • Payment information: information necessary to process or reconcile a payment. Where a payment provider processes card or mobile-money details directly, we may receive transaction status and reference information rather than the underlying payment credentials.
  • Customer communications: enquiries, complaints, reviews, survey responses and other information you choose to provide.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device information, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, error information and security logs.
  • Cookie and usage information: information collected through cookies and similar technologies, subject to your cookie choices where consent is required.
4. Google Sign-In and Google user data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google authenticates you and provides us with the information permitted by the Google sign-in scopes and your authorization. For a normal sign-in flow, this may include your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier associated with the sign-in.

We use Google-provided information only to:

  • create or authenticate your Dalali Winehouse account;
  • associate your Google sign-in with the correct customer account;
  • maintain account security and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized access; and
  • communicate with you about your account, orders or services where necessary.

We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, sell it to data brokers, or use it for unrelated purposes. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts or other Google services unless a separate feature is introduced and its data use is disclosed before access is requested.

We do not receive or store your Google password. Google authentication occurs through Google's authorization systems.

Our handling of information received through Google APIs is intended to comply with Google's API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. We will keep this disclosure current if the Google sign-in implementation or requested scopes change.

5. How and when we collect information

We collect information when you create or use an account, sign in, place an order, request delivery, contact customer service, submit a review or enquiry, subscribe to communications, or otherwise interact with the website.

We also automatically receive certain technical information when your browser or device connects to our website. We may receive limited information from service providers that help us operate authentication, payments, delivery, hosting, security and analytics.

6. Why we use personal information
  • to provide the website, accounts and services you request;
  • to process, fulfil and deliver orders;
  • to authenticate users and maintain account security;
  • to respond to enquiries, complaints and support requests;
  • to process and reconcile payments and maintain transaction records;
  • to maintain, troubleshoot and improve the website and its security;
  • to comply with applicable legal, regulatory, tax and accounting requirements;
  • to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity; and
  • where permitted and, where required, with your consent, to send marketing communications and measure their effectiveness.

We will not use Google user data for a purpose that is inconsistent with the disclosures in this Privacy Policy.

7. Legal grounds and your choices

Depending on the circumstances, we process personal information because it is necessary to provide a service or perform a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect our legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using an unsubscribe mechanism provided in the communication or contacting us.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may share personal information with service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, website and software infrastructure, authentication, payment processing, delivery, customer support, security, accounting and professional services.

We may also disclose information where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where necessary to protect our rights, customers, systems or property.

Service providers should only process information for the services they provide to us and subject to appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, we will take steps required by applicable data-protection law to provide appropriate safeguards, including relying on applicable contractual, legal or consent-based mechanisms where required.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational and administrative measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. These measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, logging, backups and security monitoring.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you believe your account or interaction with us has been compromised, contact customer service promptly.

11. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, order fulfilment, customer support, security, dispute resolution and legal, tax and accounting obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it where appropriate, subject to lawful retention requirements.

You may request deletion of your account or personal information by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

12. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to be informed about processing, access your personal information, object to certain processing, request correction of inaccurate information and request deletion where the law permits. You may also have additional rights under applicable data-protection law.

To exercise a right, contact our customer-service team using the details on this website. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies and may use functional, analytics or other non-essential cookies where permitted and, where required, after obtaining your consent.

Cookies may support login sessions, shopping baskets, website functionality, security, analytics and preferences. Third-party services may also set cookies when their features are enabled.

You can manage cookies through available website controls and your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, legal obligations or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date. Where a material change affects how we use Google user data, we will update the relevant disclosure before or when the changed processing begins, as appropriate.
