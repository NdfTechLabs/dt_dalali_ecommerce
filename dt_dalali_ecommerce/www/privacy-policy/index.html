Privacy Policy

Dalali Winehouse Ltd · Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Last updated: 15 October 2026

About this policy. This Privacy Policy explains how Dalali Winehouse Ltd collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information when you browse our website, create or use an account, place an order, contact us, or choose Google Sign-In. Please also read our Terms & Conditions.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Dalali Winehouse Ltd (“Dalali”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates the Dalali Winehouse online storefront at dalaliwinehouseltd.com and provides related customer services.

This policy applies to personal information collected through our website, customer accounts, product browsing, orders, enquiries, customer-support interactions and authentication services used by the website.

For privacy enquiries or requests concerning your personal information, use the contact details in section 15.

2. Legal purchase age and responsible access

Our products include alcoholic beverages. You must meet the legal purchase age applicable to the transaction and jurisdiction before purchasing alcohol through our services.

Where required by law or by the operation of our services, we may use information necessary to support age or access controls. We do not knowingly seek personal information from people who are not legally permitted to purchase alcohol.

3. Personal information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Dalali. It may include:

  • Identity and contact information: name, email address, telephone number, billing address and delivery address.
  • Account information: account identifier, sign-in method, account preferences and information needed to maintain account security.
  • Google account information: where you choose Google Sign-In and grant the relevant permission, basic profile information such as your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier, as made available by Google and requested by our sign-in implementation.
  • Order information: products viewed, items added to a basket, order details, delivery information, invoices and transaction references.
  • Payment information: information needed to process or reconcile payments. Where a payment provider processes card or mobile-money credentials directly, we may receive transaction status and reference information rather than the underlying credentials.
  • Customer communications: enquiries, complaints, reviews and information you choose to provide to customer support.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, browser and device information, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, error information, security logs and cookie-related information.

4. Google Sign-In and Google user data

Information we receive

If you choose to create or access a Dalali account using Google Sign-In, Google provides the account information permitted by the sign-in permissions we request. Depending on the permissions and implementation, this may include your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate your identity, create or access your Dalali account, associate your Google identity with the appropriate Dalali account, help protect account security and provide account-related services. We do not receive your Google password through Google Sign-In.

Storage and retention

Information received through Google Sign-In may be stored in Dalali's account and application records to the extent necessary for the account and services described in this policy. We retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, subject to applicable legal, security, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements. When it is no longer required, we will delete or securely dispose of it where appropriate, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We may process or disclose the minimum information necessary to service providers that support our website hosting, application infrastructure, authentication, security and customer support, where those providers need the information to provide services to Dalali. We do not disclose Google user data to third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use Google Sign-In and may use another sign-in method made available by the website. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your account by contacting us using the details in section 15. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dalali Winehouse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The specific Google information we receive is determined by the permissions requested by our actual sign-in implementation. We do not intend to use Google user data for advertising or for purposes unrelated to providing and securing the account features described above.

5. How we collect information

We collect information when you browse the website, create or use an account, sign in, place an order, request delivery, contact customer service, submit a review or enquiry, or otherwise interact with Dalali.

We also receive certain technical information when your browser or device connects to our website. Service providers may provide information necessary for authentication, payment processing, delivery, hosting, security and analytics, depending on which services are enabled.

6. Why we use personal information

Subject to applicable law, we may use personal information to:

  • provide the website, customer accounts and services you request;
  • authenticate users and maintain account security;
  • process, fulfil and deliver orders;
  • respond to enquiries, complaints and support requests;
  • process payments and maintain transaction records;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve website functionality and security;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity;
  • comply with applicable legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations; and
  • send marketing communications where permitted and, where required, with your consent.

We will not use Google user data for purposes inconsistent with this policy or Google's applicable user-data requirements.

7. Legal grounds and your choices

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we process personal information because it is necessary to provide a service or perform a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable. You may opt out of marketing communications using an unsubscribe mechanism provided in the communication or by contacting us.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may share personal information with service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as hosting and software infrastructure, authentication, payment processing, delivery, customer support, security, accounting and professional services. They may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and subject to appropriate legal or contractual safeguards.

We may also disclose information where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, customers, systems or property.

We do not sell Google user data or permit service providers to use it for their own independent advertising purposes.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, we will take steps required by applicable data-protection law to provide appropriate safeguards.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational and administrative measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. Measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, logging, backups and security monitoring.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you believe your account or interaction with Dalali has been compromised, contact us promptly.

11. Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, order fulfilment, customer support, security, dispute resolution and legal, tax and accounting obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it where appropriate, subject to lawful retention requirements.

You may request deletion of your account or personal information by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

12. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to be informed about processing, access your personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing, or request deletion where the law permits. You may have additional rights under applicable data-protection law.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details in section 15. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies and may use functional, analytics or other non-essential cookies where permitted and, where required, after obtaining your consent.

Cookies may support login sessions, shopping baskets, website functionality, security, analytics and preferences. Third-party services may also set cookies when their features are enabled.

You can manage cookies through available website controls and your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect website functionality.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, legal obligations or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date. Where a material change affects how we use Google user data, we will update the relevant disclosure before or when the changed processing begins, as appropriate.
