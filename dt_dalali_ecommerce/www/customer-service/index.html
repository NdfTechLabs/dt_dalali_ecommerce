Customer Service

Dalali Winehouse Ltd · Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

We are here to help. This page explains how to contact Dalali Winehouse for order assistance, account support, delivery questions, complaints and other customer-service matters.
1. Contact channels
  • Email: customercare@dalaliwinehouse.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +254 728 583 967
  • Website: dalaliwinehouseltd.com

When contacting us about an order, include your order number and the name or contact details used for the order where possible. Do not send passwords, full card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information by email or WhatsApp.

2. Order assistance

Our customer-service team can assist with order status, product availability, delivery details, payment confirmation, account access and other questions relating to your purchase.

We may need to verify certain account or order details before discussing information about an order.

3. Google Sign-In and account support

If you created or accessed your account using Google Sign-In and are unable to sign in, contact customer service using the details above. We may ask for information needed to identify your Dalali account, but we will never ask for your Google password.

4. Delivery support

If a delivery is delayed, incomplete, damaged or cannot be completed, contact us as soon as reasonably possible with your order number and relevant details.

For age-restricted deliveries, the recipient may be required to satisfy applicable age or identity checks before the order can be handed over.

5. Complaints and service issues

We encourage customers to contact us first so that we can investigate and resolve service issues. Please provide a clear description of the problem, order number, relevant dates and supporting photographs where appropriate.

Complaints involving damaged, incorrect or defective products should be reported promptly and may be handled under our Refund & Returns Policy and any mandatory rights available under applicable law.

6. Privacy and data requests

Privacy, access, correction, objection and deletion requests are handled under our Privacy Policy. We may verify your identity before responding to a request concerning personal information.

7. Response times

We aim to respond to customer enquiries as soon as reasonably practicable. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request, delivery location, payment provider or other third-party dependency.

8. Contact details

Email: customercare@dalaliwinehouse.com
Phone / WhatsApp: +254 728 583 967

Before publication, confirm that these contact details are current.
